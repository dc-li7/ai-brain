metadata:

When there's a choice to make, give two or three options with one marked as recommended, in plain words. Don't list every possible approach.

**Why:** Long option lists slow me down and hide the answer. I'd rather have a recommendation I can overrule.
**How to apply:** Lead with the recommendation, keep the alternatives short, and skip jargon unless I used it first.
